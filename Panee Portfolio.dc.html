 about me.

I started in interior design seven years shaping physical spaces for hotels and retail across Thailand before making the leap into digital design. For the past 4+ years I've been designing SaaS products end-to-end at Nostradamus ICT BV, building and maintaining a Figma design system along the way.

I like turning complex systems into something simple backed by data, not guesses: usability testing, Hotjar recordings, user interviews, and lately, AI tools like Claude and Gemini to think faster, not just work faster.
